How it works

Three gestures. Nothing to learn.

People already know how to swipe. Easy Swipe turns that habit into a faster way to browse a catalogue on a phone.

  1. Right to add

    Swipe right, or tap Add, and it goes straight into the bag. Quantities and checkout live in the bag drawer.

  2. Left to skip

    Not for them? It's gone and won't come back this visit. Changed their mind? Undo is always one tap away.

  3. Up to save

    Not today, but maybe. Saved products wait in their own list and move to the bag with one tap.

Why stores use it

Built around your store, not ours.

  • Your checkout stays yours

    When a shopper taps Checkout, Easy Swipe hands you the bag: items, quantities, subtotal and currency. Your existing checkout and payment provider take it from there. We never handle payments.

  • See what people actually want

    A plain dashboard shows every add, save and skip, product by product. Find the items people love, and the ones they keep passing over.

  • English and Arabic, properly

    A full right-to-left layout, Arabic product names and descriptions, and prices in SAR, AED, USD or any other currency.

  • Fits any store

    Point it at a JSON, CSV or Google Shopping feed. It runs in its own sandbox, so it never clashes with your theme, and it stays under 30 KB.

Install

Live on your store in an afternoon.

  1. Add the script

    Paste one tag before </body>. No framework needed.

  2. Point it at your feed

    Use the product feed you already have for Google Shopping, or a JSON or CSV export.

  3. Connect checkout

    Listen for one event and send the bag to your own checkout.

<!-- Easy Swipe -->
<script src="https://cdn.easyswipe.app/v1/easy-swipe.js"
        data-store-id="your-store"
        data-feed-url="/feeds/google-shopping.xml"
        data-currency="SAR"></script>
// Easy Swipe hands you the bag. You run checkout.
window.addEventListener('easyswipe:checkout', (event) => {
  const { items, subtotal, currency } = event.detail;
  myStore.cart.add(items).then(() => location.assign('/checkout'));
});

// Open it from any button on your site
EasySwipe.open();
